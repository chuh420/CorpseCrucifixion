title=Corpse Crucifixion
description=[h1]Corpse Crucifixion[/h1]
description=Nail a corpse to a wall, a fence, a tree or a wooden cross you build yourself.
description=
description=[h2]How it works[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Right-click a corpse and pick [b]Crucify Corpse[/b]. You need a hammer and 4 nails.
description=[*]Move the cursor to choose the spot. The body preview turns green where it fits and red where it does not. Press R to turn it.
description=[*]Your character walks over and nails the body up. It keeps its face, hair, skin, clothes and backpack.
description=[*]Loot the body while it hangs, or right-click it and pick [b]Take Down Corpse[/b]. The same body drops back to the ground and you get your 4 nails back.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Wooden cross[/h2]
description=Craft it in the Carpentry section from 2 planks and 2 nails with a hammer. Right-click it in your inventory and pick [b]Place Cross[/b] to set it up with a preview and rotation, like furniture. You can pick it back up while nobody hangs on it.
description=
description=[h2]Notes[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Made for Build 42. Multiplayer support is untested.
description=[*]You can add the mod to an existing save.
description=[*]Take the bodies down before you remove the mod, or you lose them.
description=[/list]
